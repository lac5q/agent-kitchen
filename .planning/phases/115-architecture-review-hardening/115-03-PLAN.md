# 115-03 Plan: Planning History Retention Decision

## Objective

Complete `ARCHREV-08` by deciding how planning history is retained before wider release without deleting GSD context or moving private artifacts without Luis approval.

## Decision

Planning history remains durable in git. Current roadmap files stay in place, completed historical phase internals move to `.planning/archive/phases/` when pruning is needed, and screenshots or operational evidence must move to a private sibling repo or receive explicit public approval before wider release.

Canonical policy: `.planning/planning-history-retention.md`.

## Non-Goals

- No destructive deletion.
- No private sibling repo writes in this slice.
- No public-release claim for existing screenshots or audit internals.
