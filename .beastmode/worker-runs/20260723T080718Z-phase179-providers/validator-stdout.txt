### Critical finding — `appendActivityEvent` writes to nonexistent columns
`credential-store.ts:285-296` inserts into `audit_entries (event_type, actor_type, actor_id, target_type, target_id, ...)`, but the actual Phase-64 schema (`db-schema.ts:3455`) has **no** `actor_type`, `target_type`, or `target_id` columns, and omits the NOT-NULL `id`, `actor_role`, `entity_type`, `entity_id`. `better-sqlite3` throws at `.prepare()`. Because the function isn't wrapped in try/catch (unlike the correct pattern at `artifact-gate.ts:75`), the throw propagates and **500s all three write endpoints**:
- **api-key connect**: vault write succeeds, then throws → misleading `vault_write_failed` 500
- **disconnect**: vault row deleted, then throws → 500 (revoke reports failure after succeeding)
- **oauth connect**: session created, then throws → 500
- **Recent activity strip is permanently empty** (writes never land)

### Why CI missed it
`credential-store.test.ts` only has 2 tests, and they call `writeVaultArtifact`/`readVaultArtifact` **directly** — they never exercise the module's own `appendActivityEvent`/`listActivityEvents`/`storeApiKeyConnection`. Typecheck can't catch a raw SQL string. That's the exact blind spot.
