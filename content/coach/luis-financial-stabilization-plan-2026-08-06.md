# Luis — Financial Stabilization and Income Plan

## Immediate frame

This is a stabilization period, not a verdict on Luis's capability or future. The provisional priority is to protect the next one to two weeks, reduce preventable creditor escalation, and restore an income pipeline.

If hopelessness becomes an immediate safety risk or includes thoughts of self-harm, contact local emergency services, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the United States, and a trusted person who can stay nearby.

## What is known

- Business credit-card payments cannot be made this week.
- Creditor calls need to begin early next week.
- A job search is urgent, but financial stress is making sustained focus difficult.
- A large agentic-integration opportunity was recently lost.
- Luis has been investing significant attention in AI systems and the Coach itself.
- Prior self-report indicates a preference for direct feedback with care, a need for a clear why before a what, and a risk that busy activity can displace vital work.

## What remains unknown

Do not infer these values:

- cash available and confirmed incoming cash;
- each card's issuer, balance, minimum payment, and due date;
- whether any card has a personal guarantee;
- payroll, taxes, rent, insurance, food, housing, or other essential obligations;
- the exact job titles, compensation floor, location constraints, and resume readiness;
- today's numeric focus, energy, stress, vital minutes, or busy minutes.

- Outcome: Stabilize near-term finances and secure dependable income.
- Target behavior: Complete one financial-protection action and one focused job-search action each weekday.
- Why now: Business cards cannot be paid this week; creditor outreach is due early next week; financial stress is impairing focus.
- Source-purpose permission: self-report for coaching.
- Mode: advisory only.
- Baseline: seven days.
- Status: written to the local Coach store on 2026-08-06; correct or replace it if it does not fit.

## The next 30 minutes

Do only one task: create a creditor call sheet. Do not solve the debt or start rebuilding the Coach.

For each card, record:

1. Issuer and callback number from the statement.
2. Due date and minimum payment.
3. Current balance.
4. Personal guarantee: yes, no, or unknown.
5. Autopay status.
6. Space for representative name, case number, offered terms, and written-confirmation date.

Stop after 30 minutes even if the sheet is incomplete.

## Creditor call script

> I am calling before a missed payment because the business has a short-term cash-flow problem. I want to understand every hardship, workout, temporary-payment, fee, or interest option available before the account becomes further delinquent. I cannot promise an amount the business cannot support. Please explain the payment amount and dates, fees and interest, account-use restrictions, reporting consequences, and whether you can send the terms in writing.

Ask specifically:

- Is there a hardship or workout team?
- Can the due date, minimum, fees, or interest be adjusted temporarily?
- What happens if the offered payment is made, and what happens if it is not?
- Will the card be frozen or closed?
- How will the account be reported?
- Can the complete terms be provided in writing?
- What is the representative's name and case number?

Contact issuers as early as possible. CFPB consumer guidance recommends contacting an issuer as soon as a payment problem is known. Business-purpose cards, however, are exempt from many consumer-credit rules, so the card agreement and issuer's written terms matter. If personal guarantees, taxes, payroll, insolvency, or shutdown are involved, use a qualified CPA or attorney rather than relying on the Coach.

SBA Resource Partners, including SBDCs and SCORE, provide free or low-cost business counseling and can help evaluate options without relying solely on a creditor's sales or collections representative.

## Seven-day job-search operating rhythm

The search has two lanes.

### Lane 1 — fastest credible income

Use warm relationships first: former clients, partners, colleagues, investors, and people from the agentic-integration process. Ask about immediate contract work, fractional work, and full-time roles. Do not imply the lost deal was won.

Suggested positioning:

> I am available for immediate work helping organizations move agentic-AI efforts from prototype to integrated delivery. My useful edge is connecting technical design, stakeholder needs, implementation planning, and delivery risk. I am considering hands-on consulting, solutions/implementation leadership, and full-time roles. If a team in your network needs this now, I would appreciate an introduction.

### Lane 2 — stable role

Start with three role families rather than the whole market:

- AI solutions architect or solutions engineering;
- AI implementation, delivery, or technical program leadership;
- applied AI product or consulting roles.

Treat these as provisional until the resume and constraints are reviewed.

### Daily minimum

Complete these before working on Coach infrastructure or broad AI research:

1. One 25-minute warm-network block: send three specific messages.
2. One 25-minute application block: submit one tailored application.
3. One 10-minute follow-up block: update the tracker and choose tomorrow's first contact.

A completed small output is the metric. Hours spent researching or perfecting materials do not count.

American Job Centers provide free job-search help, career counseling, resume assistance, interview practice, and local hiring information. Use one if the search remains hard to organize or if practical assistance would reduce the burden.

## If-then plan

If Luis notices himself improving the Coach, researching tools, or polishing materials before completing the day's money action and job-search action, then pause and complete ten minutes of the earliest unfinished cash or outreach task.

## Stop and adjust conditions

- Stop a creditor call if pressure or confusion makes the terms unclear; request written terms and review them with a qualified professional.
- Do not accept a payment promise that cannot be met.
- Do not take new high-cost debt solely to avoid the discomfort of a creditor conversation.
- Reduce the daily job-search minimum if it repeatedly triggers shutdown; preserve one warm message and one application rather than abandoning the search.
- Reassess after seven days using completed actions, responses, interviews, cash facts, and stress—not self-judgment.
