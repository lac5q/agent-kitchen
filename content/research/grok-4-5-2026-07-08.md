## Canonical Facts

### Release
- **Public launch:** July 8, 2026 announcement, July 9, 2026 public availability
- **Private beta:** Started June 28, 2026 at SpaceX and Tesla
- **Rebrand:** xAI → SpaceXAI in early July 2026, post SpaceX merger

### Architecture
- Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)
- V9 foundation, ~1.5T parameters (self-reported)
- Trained jointly with Cursor on trillions of tokens of Cursor user-interaction data (supplemental training, not pre-training)
- Trained on tens of thousands of NVIDIA GB300 GPUs in Memphis data centers

### Pricing
- $2.00/M input, $6.00/M output, $0.50/M cached input (75% discount)
- 500K context window (with tiered pricing above 200K)
- Cursor fast variant: $4/$18 per million tokens
