# Luis Calderon UVP Adversarial Validation

## Verdict

DEFENSIBLE-WITH-FIXES.

The braid is real enough to use: enterprise-scale growth product work, owner-operator credibility, and hands-on AI systems. The current sentence is not ready for executive search because it tries to win too many arguments at once. A skeptical VP Product reader will not reject the core story, but they will challenge attribution, inconsistent scale numbers, and the leap from "built agentic job-search tools" to "AI-native executive."

## The 3 Strongest Attacks

1. Attribution and scale will get challenged in reference calls.

The riskiest phrase is "$900M-$1B Intuit P&L he grew 10-15% a year." If Luis did not directly own the P&L, pricing, full GTM, and business result, this should not say "P&L he grew." It should say "owned product strategy for a $900M+ TurboTax Self-Employed business that grew 10-15% annually" or similarly bounded language. eBay "+2% checkout conversion" and "$100M+ lift" also need a clear attribution line: what did Luis own, what did the team own, and what would a reference confirm?

2. The AI claim reads current, but not yet externally proven.

"AI doing the labor," "AI-native executive," and "personally automated a company with AI" sound like 2026 AI-branding language. The artifacts help, but a hiring manager will separate personal workflow automation from enterprise AI product leadership. ApplyPilot, Chrome extensions, eval harnesses, and pipeline-runs prove builder energy and product discipline. They do not by themselves prove a company-wide AI operating model unless tied to production users, cost savings, cycle-time reduction, quality metrics, or P&L.

3. SketchPop is a double-edged proof point.

The owner-operator strand is valuable, but it can also make the profile look like a small-business operator trying to re-enter enterprise product. "$3M revenue, 30 people, 20% EBITDA" is useful only if the story is disciplined: acquired and operated the company, owned product/CX/backend ops, shipped AI workflows, cut service costs 40%. The draft should not imply breakout growth if the known weakness is flat growth. The proof point is operating leverage, not growth-stage scaling.

- Intuit: $900M+ business context, 4M+ customers, 4 PMs, regulated fintech, AI/ML product work, and 10-15% annual growth if verified.
