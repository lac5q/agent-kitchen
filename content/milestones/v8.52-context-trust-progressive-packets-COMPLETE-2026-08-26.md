# v8.52 Context Trust + Progressive Packets — COMPLETE (2026-08-26)

All 8 phases (258–265) landed on `main`. Built via GSD beastmode: Opus director
(phase plans + gates + diff review + merges), Sonnet executors (isolated
worktrees, incremental commits). Governance translation of the Glean/OpenViking
research doc (PR #25) — MemroOS does NOT become a Glean clone or swap its memory
stack; every phase extends an existing subsystem.

## What each phase shipped

- **258 CTXREF** — one frozen `ContextRef` contract; 3 deterministic
  representations (card/overview/full); truth (belief stage + label) carried,
  never invented; redaction survives projection.
- **259 CTXPKT** — recall manifest + budget-aware depth selection; deterministic
  selection; explicit deepening; ships behind a flag (default-off until Phase 237
  measurement).
- **260 TRUSTLED** (`b74d53d7`) — source-to-answer trust ledger: source
  identity/revisions, freshness+deletion on every candidate, evidence-computed
  trust badge, reconciliation totals, ACL snapshots, citations. Adversarial
  canaries CAUGHT + fixed a real cross-tenant leak in reconciliation totals.
  Migrations 62–64 append-only.
- **261 ADOPT1** — one-command adoption with a verified write→recall→cite
  round-trip canary; citations wired into `/api/memory/add` + `/api/recall`
  (additive, fail-open). "Onboarded" now means the credential actually works.
- **262 CTXPROOF** — competitive proof suite on retrieval-bench:
  freshness/deletion + permission dimensions (reusing P260) + a publication gate
  encoding the sequencing lock (no comparative trust claim unless P260 is green).
- **263 RJRNY** — retrieval journey debugger + `/memory/journey` dashboard;
  every candidate with a derived verdict; policy refusals called out separately
  from low relevance; fail-open recording.
- **264 CTXCOMP** — governed compilation of briefs/wikis/playbooks as reviewable
  drafts; single-writer no-auto-promotion; capability-gated human approve;
  per-section citations with unciteable markers.
- **265 CONNSDK** — connector SDK + certification. `ConnectorManifest` now
  REQUIRES a completeness claim + permission model; `certifyConnector` fails any
  connector lacking a published declaration (slack/github today), a fabricated
  `entire-tenant` claim, or a governance-surface declaration drifted from the
  live ceiling; `certifiedConnectorRegistry` gives a defensible count (certified
  6 < 8 raw). Encodes non-goal 4 in code.

## Binding non-goals honored

No storage-model replacement; no intranet-search product before the agent-context
loop is excellent; no auto-promotion of summaries/artifacts to gold (P256 rule);
no connector counted without published completeness+permission (265); no benchmark
claim without a runnable harness + baseline (262); filesystem metaphor is
addressing not authorization.
