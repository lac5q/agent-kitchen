| Time | Required beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3s | Selected hook; face and/or source photo visible; disclosure already clear |
| 3–7s | Brief ordering explanation: choose/upload a photo and place the portrait order |
| 7–15s | Portrait reveal and authentic recipient/creator reaction |
| 15–22s | Why it works as a personalized holiday gift; show useful portrait detail |
| 22–30s | CTA: “Create yours with Turned Yellow”; show unique link/code |

## Do

- Say “Turned Yellow” as the ordinary English words “turned” and “yellow.”
- Use your genuine experience and only make claims you can substantiate.
- Put `Ad:` or `#ad` at the beginning of the caption and show a readable on-screen disclosure during the endorsement; say it aloud where practical. Required plain-language disclosure: “Turned Yellow gave me this portrait and pays me if you buy through my link.” If the fixed bounty applies, use “Turned Yellow paid me, gave me this portrait, and pays me if you buy through my link.”
- Use TikTok’s content-disclosure setting or Meta’s paid-partnership label in addition to the in-content disclosure.
- Obtain written consent from every identifiable person; creators must be 18+ and minors may appear only with verified guardian consent.
- Record clean dialogue, strong front lighting, stable vertical footage, and uncluttered portrait detail.
- Keep the live post public for at least 90 days and preserve rights evidence for 12 months.
- Use only original voice/room sound or music with written paid-commercial-use clearance; a music-free file is preferred.

## Don’t

- Don’t hide disclosure after “more,” in comments, among hashtags, or only in a platform label.
- Don’t claim guaranteed delivery dates, quality outcomes, discounts, or product features not supplied in the approved claim sheet.
- Don’t mention or show competitors, copyrighted characters/artwork, TV/audio, logos, private information, order details, or bystanders without clearance.
- Don’t use trending audio merely because the platform offers it; organic availability is not paid-ad clearance.
