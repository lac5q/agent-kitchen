The historic JP recording and exact wording were not recovered. The earlier label “JP-style” was a reconstruction based on the operator's later instruction to start a $100–$200 JoinBrands test. It should not be treated as proof that JP recommended one $50 fixed-fee Instagram Reel.

This remains an inference until the JP source is recovered.

## JoinBrands mechanics

- Standard Instagram `Content + Posting` requires a fixed base price of at least $50 per Reel. Selecting this type does not necessarily mean only one Reel; creator count is configured later.
- `Social Blasts` is the platform's volume format, using packages of 10, 25, 50, or 100 posts. Exact Instagram package pricing must be confirmed in the authenticated builder. The full expected package budget is charged at purchase and is non-refundable.
- JoinBrands supports optional performance bonuses for Content + Posting and Blast campaigns. Official documentation describes metric/goal/reward milestones and a tracking window up to five days after the final job; it does not establish a continuous CPM payout contract.
- Therefore the closest in-platform translation of a $2.50 CPM test is a milestone such as **10,000 views = $25 bonus**, not “$2.50 per million views.” The authenticated builder must confirm that views are available as the metric and show the full maximum liability.

Proceed only if the full non-refundable package, bonus exposure, platform fee, card fee, tax, and product cost fit the approved cap.
