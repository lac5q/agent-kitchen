## Contract mechanics that matter

1. An adjusted weekly amount lasts fourteen days.
2. Continued relief requires new bank statements supporting the adjustment every fourteen days.
3. Without updated statements, the weekly amount reverts to the Key Terms amount of $1,712.50.
4. The contract provides a monthly reconciliation process for amounts collected above 14% of actual Future Receipts. A request must be made within ninety days after the relevant month, and the agreement says the overage should be credited within ten business days after a valid request.
5. Two consecutive missed weekly payments convert the schedule to five daily debits per week.
6. A returned ACH can incur a $35 fee. Four consecutive returns without timely financial documentation can constitute a breach.
7. Intentionally blocking the approved account can trigger $2,500 in stated damages plus other remedies. Do not block debits or redirect receipts without legal advice.
8. The email says payments continue through March 24, 2027 if nothing changes and payments occur as scheduled, but it lists amounts only through October 7. The complete remaining schedule and current uncollected balance are not provided.
