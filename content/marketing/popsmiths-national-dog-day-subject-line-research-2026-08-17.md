# Recommendation

Use:

- **Subject:** `National Dog Day, gallery-ready.`
- **Preheader:** `Turn one favorite dog photo into art across 14 dedicated pet styles.`
- **Hero:** `THIS NATIONAL DOG DAY, MAKE THEM GALLERY-READY.`

# Why this is the strongest option

The subject is four words and approximately 32 characters. It puts the timely occasion first, communicates the campaign's visual payoff, and should display substantially or completely in common mobile inbox views. It uses no emoji, exclamation point, artificial urgency, or unsupported offer language.

Current Omnisend guidance recommends putting the key message in the first 40–50 characters and using clear, relevant language. Omnisend's broader optimization guidance recommends five to seven words, while SendGrid's proprietary observations favor roughly three to four words. Klaviyo's campaign research also finds that shorter subjects outperform increasingly wordy variants and cites roughly seven words or fewer than 36 characters as a useful ceiling. This candidate satisfies the stricter end of all three ranges.

The preheader adds concrete detail instead of repeating the subject. It explains the one-photo transformation and the 14-style breadth, giving the inbox pair both emotional relevance and product specificity.

# Ranked alternatives

1. `National Dog Day, gallery-ready.` — best balance of occasion, brevity, relevance, and brand fit.
2. `Make National Dog Day a masterpiece` — clear benefit and six words, but "masterpiece" is less distinctive and risks sounding inflated.
3. `Their National Dog Day portrait awaits` — personal and curiosity-led, but "their" is initially ambiguous.
4. `National Dog Day starts with one photo` — explains the mechanism, but is longer and less emotionally vivid.
5. `A portrait for National Dog Day` — very clear, but comparatively generic.

# Operational note

The only reliable way to know the list-specific winner is an A/B test. For an urgent same-day send, the available send window and audience size make a prolonged winner test impractical. Use the recommended subject now, then compare delivered, unique-open, click, and conversion results against future PopSmiths campaigns. Apple Mail Privacy Protection means open rate should be treated as a directional measure rather than the sole success metric.
