**Agentic Orchestration for Regulated Fintech (Memroos):**
- Origin: built Memroos at SketchPop to govern agent behavior in a highly regulated commerce stack; positioned it as an agentic orchestration tool for fintech products in highly regulated industries, where non-deterministic LLM outcomes carry audit, compliance, and customer-trust risk
- **Evals**: offline eval suites and online scoring tied to task success, tool-selection correctness, and customer-impact metrics; eval failures promote a prompt or skill change through a gated path rather than straight to prod
- **Gating**: human-in-the-loop checkpoints and policy gates before agents touch money, identity, or compliance-bearing state; approval workflows distinguish destructive actions from reversible ones
- **Determinism tactics**: structured tool contracts, retrieval as the source of truth, deterministic pre/post-conditions on every tool call, idempotent operations, replayable traces, and budgeted retry with backoff
- **Predictability**: every agent run emits structured lineage for audit, regression detection, and post-incident root cause; satisfaction, error patterns, and human handoff rates roll up into dashboards that decide whether autonomy is expanded or rolled back
