## Important evidence boundaries

- Reimagined Expenses is the best-documented Intuit story. The exact onboarding-video lift is not in the current corpus.
- QuickFile targets are forecasts. Do not claim that Luis achieved 90,000 starts or a two-point completion lift without a final readout.
- The Divergent New Start results are program results within Luis's documented ownership lanes. Do not imply sole ownership.
- Grace Rieger owned detailed execution for parts of Specialized Experts. Use that work as team-leadership evidence unless a Luis-owned source establishes direct execution.
- The SketchPop narrative must say acquired and operated. Do not say Luis founded the original custom-art business or built it from zero.
- The old Doctor.com resume says the reporting platform increased engagement 15% and "decreased retention." Treat the retention wording as an apparent error until Luis confirms whether it meant increased retention or decreased churn.
- The Tile source says launch was successful but provides no numeric outcome.
- The eBay source records the three metrics but does not show whether they came from one program or several releases.
