## Provisional top three

1. Intuit — Reimagined Self-Employed Expenses.
2. Intuit SOM — Divergent Start, Checkout, and Pricing.
3. SketchPop — End-to-End Business Transformation.

Tile is the fourth story and eBay is fifth. For subscription, marketplace, payments, or business-model roles, use Tile or eBay as the first backup. For AI transformation roles, use SketchPop AI-Supported Operations.

## Important evidence boundaries

- Reimagined Expenses is the best-documented Intuit story. Frame the win as a segment strategy and team rebuild, not only a new expense flow.
- Treat SketchPop's 90% figure as the revenue coverage of the rebuilt experiences unless Luis confirms that it was an experiment lift.
- Treat Tile's approximately $10M-to-$100M company growth as context. Attribute the approximately 30% revenue contribution directly to Luis's subscription program.
- Keep eBay's UPI result separate from the broader 2% conversion, 33% defect reduction, and $100M-plus release metrics until Luis confirms their relationship.
- Interpret the eBay credit work as seller fee-credit recovery unless Luis supplies a different product name.
- QuickFile targets are forecasts. Do not claim that Luis achieved 90,000 starts or a two-point completion lift without a final readout.
- The archive records SOM indices of 105 and 122 and aspirational recommendations below 6%. Luis's 5%, 22%, and 6% figures are firsthand and need unit confirmation before final scripts.
- Grace Rieger owned detailed execution for parts of Specialized Experts. Use that work as team-leadership evidence unless a Luis-owned source establishes direct execution.
- The SketchPop narrative must say acquired and operated. Do not say Luis founded the original custom-art business or built it from zero.
- The old Doctor.com resume says the reporting platform increased engagement 15% and "decreased retention." Treat the retention wording as an apparent error until Luis confirms whether it meant increased retention or decreased churn.

## Interview questions

1. SketchPop: does 90% mean revenue coverage or A/B-test lift?
2. eBay: what is the denominator for the 8% revenue result, and what was the exact name of the seller-credit product?
3. SOM: are the 5%, 22%, and 6% results percentage changes, percentage-point changes, or indices?
4. Tile: was the program called ReTile, and does 30% refer to revenue, bookings, or unit sales?
5. SketchPop chatbot: does 30-plus mean customers, conversations, or share of contacts?
