# Master TMAY and Interview Story Library

## Canonical practice document

- [Luis Calderon — Master TMAY & Interview Story Library](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RzOjy40X9SQ1biO7IZgM3R00i6YPxKyJhBf74VmfZrA/edit)

This is the single working document for interview practice. It contains a 30-second opener, a 150-to-250-word TMAY-style script, use guidance, and evidence boundaries for every story.

## Structure

### Strategic stories

1. Intuit — Reimagined Self-Employed
2. Intuit SOM — Divergent Start, Checkout, and Pricing
3. SketchPop — End-to-End Business Transformation
4. Tile — Hardware as a Service
5. eBay — Marketplace Transaction Completion and Seller Economics
6. Ancestry — ML-Personalized SEO Acquisition
7. Doctor.com — Personalized Provider Reporting

### Focused drill-downs

8. Reimagined Expenses, nested under Reimagined Self-Employed
9. QuickFile, 1099-K, and partner data, nested under Reimagined Self-Employed
10. SketchPop AI-supported operations, nested under the SketchPop transformation

## Validation

Claude Opus 4.8 reviewed the final local source with `claude -p --model opus`. It passed these checks:

- all ten full scripts are within the 150-to-250-word TMAY range;
- spoken copy uses first-person voice;
- Reimagined Self-Employed remains the umbrella and SOM remains separate;
- drill-downs are visibly nested; and
- each evidence boundary corresponds to the claims in its story card.

## Use rule

Use one full script per introduction. Do not combine multiple scripts into a TMAY. Reimagined Self-Employed is the default anchor. Select a different card only when the role strongly favors its domain, such as marketplaces for eBay or subscriptions for Tile.
